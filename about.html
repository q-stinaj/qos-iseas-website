About QOS ISEAS JV

From Surface to Seabed: Integrated IRM Solutions

About Us

QOS ISEAS JV combines the strengths of QOS Sdn. Bhd. and Integrated Subsea Engineering & Services LLC (ISEAS) to deliver fully integrated solutions across the Oil and Gas and marine sectors, anchored by a shared commitment to safety, quality, and operational excellence.

Mission

To safely and efficiently deliver diving and subsea services for BSP through an integrated JV model that combines international expertise with structured local capability development, ensuring full compliance with IMCA, IOGP, and client-specific requirements.

Vision

To be Brunei’s leading IMCA-aligned and global Shell approved commercial diving and subsea services provider, recognized for safe, reliable, and technically robust operations delivered through strong local capability and sustainable partnerships.

The Companies

QOS Sdn Bhd

Established in 2006, QOS Sdn. Bhd. is a Bruneian-owned company specializing in asset integrity management, inspection, and mechanical services for the Oil and Gas industry. With extensive experience supporting major operators such as Brunei Shell Petroleum, Brunei LNG, and Total E&P, QOS delivers advanced Non-Destructive Testing (NDT), pipeline services, and construction solutions to ensure assets remain safe, reliable, and fit-for-service throughout their lifecycle.

Integrated Subsea Engineering & Services LLC (ISEAS)

Founded in 2009, ISEAS has grown into a leading provider of subsea engineering and commercial diving services, delivering specialized underwater solutions across the global energy sector. With operations spanning multiple countries, ISEAS supports a wide range of offshore activities including diving operations, subsea installation, inspection, maintenance, ROV services, and marine support works. The company has successfully executed complex projects involving pipeline installation, offshore structures, marine infrastructure, and vessel maintenance, earning recognition for its strong QHSE culture, technical expertise, and commitment to safe, high-quality project delivery.
