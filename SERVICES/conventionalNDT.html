Conventional NDT Inspection

Conventional NDT techniques form the foundation of routine inspection and quality control processes. These include methods such as visual inspection, magnetic particle testing (MPI), penetrant testing (PT), ultrasonic testing (UT), and radiographic testing (RT).

Conventional NDT methods include:

Visual Inspection
Magnetic Particle Inspection (MPI)
Ultrasonic Thickness Gauge Testing (UTTG)
Positive Material Identification (PMI)
Ferrite Testing (FT)
Penetrant Testing (PT)
Ultrasonic Testing Flaw Detection (UT)
Radiographic Testing-Gamma Ray (RT)
Hardness Testing (HT)
Eddy Current Testing (ECT)
