Diving Solutions

ISEAS delivers expert commercial diving services to support a wide range of underwater operations, from inspection and maintenance to installation and repair. With competent, experienced personnel and advanced equipment, every project is executed with precision and strict adherence to safety standards.

Key Capabilities

  • Offshore diving operations for Oil & Gas infrastructure
  • Subsea Inspection, Repair, and Maintenance (IRM)
  • Pipeline, riser, and jacket installation support
  • Underwater welding, cutting, and construction works
  • Seabed surveys, dredging, and marine infrastructure support
  • Vessel inspection and ship husbandry services
