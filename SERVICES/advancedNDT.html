Advanced NDT Inspection

To support more complex inspections, QOS Sdn. Bhd. utilizes advanced technologies such as Phased Array Ultrasonic Testing (PAUT), Time of Flight Diffraction (TOFD), Eddy Current Testing (ECT), Acoustic Emission Testing, and Corrosion Mapping techniques. Advanced NDT is particularly critical in high-risk environments where precision and reliability are paramount, such as offshore facilities and aging infrastructure.

Advanced NDT methods include:

X-Ray Fluorescence (XRF)
Alternating Current Field Measurement (ACFM)
Vibration Testing Tools
Vacuum Box Leak Testing
Acoustic Emission for Passing Valve
Acoustic Emission Testing (MMC)
Ovality Testing for Pipes
Robotic Inspection
Automated or Robotic Corrosion Mapping
Online Tank Inspection System
Eddy Current Testing (ECT)
