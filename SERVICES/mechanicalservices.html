Mechanical Services

QOS Sdn. Bhd. offers a wide range of mechanical and pipeline services designed to support the installation, maintenance, and rehabilitation of critical infrastructure in the Oil and Gas sector. These services integrate technical expertise with practical field execution to ensure system performance, durability, and operational continuity.

Mechanical Services include:

HDPE Welding (Butt Fusion & Electro Fusion)
Rubber/HDPE/GRP/GRE Lining Services
GRE/GRV Pipe Bonding & Lamination Services
Pipe Line Flushing/Pigging (Progressive Cleaning)
Post Weld Heat Treatment (PWHT)
Waxy Cleaning & Pigging Services
Horizontal Directional Drilling (HDD)
Pre-Heating Induction
Hot Oil Flushing Works
Flange Management and Bolt Torquing
Flange Gasket Contact Resurfacing (Flange Facing)
Composite Wrapping Services
Hot Tapping & Cold Tapping
